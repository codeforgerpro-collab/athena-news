ATHENA News Room is a news-intelligence service, not a newspaper: it gathers the reporting of other newsrooms, groups it into events and explains it with numbers. Read more

Its short texts are written automatically from the linked reports, and every event leads to its original sources. Headlines and links belong to their publishers; ATHENA publishes no article text. Official warnings

What you are looking at

  • Warnings published by official bodies (governments, agencies, maritime and aviation authorities), shown as issued.

How to use it

  • Open a warning for the official text at its source; the date shows when it was issued.

Risk colours: gold under 4 · amber 4–6 · orange 6–8 · red 8 and above (scale 0–10).

ATHENA publishes an event only when at least two independent newsrooms report it.
