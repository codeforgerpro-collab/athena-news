What you are looking at

  • Every country or region with its verified events, highest risk and how its share of high-risk events compares (the coloured dot).

How to use it

  • Click a country or region to see its events.
  • Press ☆ Follow to add it to My ATHENA, or pin it to the top of the World menu there.

Risk colours: gold under 4 · amber 4–6 · orange 6–8 · red 8 and above (scale 0–10).

ATHENA publishes an event only when at least two independent newsrooms report it.
