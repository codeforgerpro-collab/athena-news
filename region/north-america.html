What you are looking at

  • The most important verified events of the moment, largest first, each with its risk (0–10) and how many newsrooms report it.

How to use it

  • Click a headline for the full event: sources, countries, risk history.
  • Use the menu for sections, the Mind Graph, Local news and Maritime.

Risk colours: gold under 4 · amber 4–6 · orange 6–8 · red 8 and above (scale 0–10).

ATHENA publishes an event only when at least two independent newsrooms report it.
