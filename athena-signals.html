How this is made

An event is a story reported by at least two independent newsrooms, in one of nine risk-assessed sections. A day is a UTC day. The risk index of a day is the average of the highest tenth of its event risks (at least three events; 0-10), so it follows the most serious events rather than the volume; high risk is 6 or more. History starts where collection really ran, and rises and falls are measured as shares of the day's events, because the archive grew in its first weeks. Similar days compare the 3-day risk index, the share of high-risk events and the peak risk, each measured against all days held. Everything is computed from the ATHENA archive when the site is published. The headline figures (gauge, section table, biggest events) use the latest 24 hours, a rolling day ending at the update time, set against every complete day held; history, charts, similar days and the track record use complete days only. Maritime counts events whose reports name ships, ports, straits or maritime bodies, and they also count in their own section.

ATHENA Signals describes past patterns in the archive. These are analytical indicators - not forecasts, advice or a prediction of any event. SGH-37

What you are looking at

  • The archive read back: the daily risk and number of events for any section, where yesterday stands in history, what is rising or fading, and a country heat calendar.

How to use it

  • Choose a section in the list at the top; every chart follows it.
  • Move across the Pulse to read each day; press Share as image to save a chart.
  • A card that says Unlocks at N days opens by itself when enough history is held.

Risk colours: gold under 4 · amber 4–6 · orange 6–8 · red 8 and above (scale 0–10).
