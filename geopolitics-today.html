What this page covers

ATHENA follows the conflicts, elections, sanctions and diplomatic moves that shape the world's risk picture. A story appears here once independent outlets in several countries have reported it, and each event carries a risk score from 0 to 10 with its reasons.

A higher score means more risk. The method is explained on the methodology page, and the Analysis Desk lets you ask where a leading story could lead.

What we watch

  • Wars and ceasefires
  • Elections and transfers of power
  • Sanctions and export controls
  • Summits, treaties and alliances
  • The Eastern Mediterranean, the Black Sea and the Middle East, closest to Greece and Cyprus
