ATHENA News Room is a news-intelligence service, not a newspaper: it gathers the reporting of other newsrooms, groups it into events and explains it with numbers. Its short texts are written automatically from the linked reports, and every event leads to its original sources. Headlines and links belong to their publishers; ATHENA publishes no article text. Scores are analytical indicators of coverage — not facts, forecasts or probabilities. Spotted an error? Write to us and we will correct it. Built automatically by ATHENA INTELLIGENCE. Local news by country

What you are looking at

  • Type any country: you get its risk picture, today's brief, the topics its own newsrooms write about and the verified events that concern it.
  • Headlines from about ten leading local newsrooms per country, machine-translated and always labelled, with the original alongside.

How to use it

  • Start typing a country name in any of the 7 languages and pick it from the list.
  • Each newsroom shows a rating by kind and reach; click a headline to read it at the source.

Risk colours: gold under 4 · amber 4–6 · orange 6–8 · red 8 and above (scale 0–10).

ATHENA publishes an event only when at least two independent newsrooms report it.
