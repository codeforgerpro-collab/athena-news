How the statuses are set

  • Disrupted: an attack, seizure, restriction or port closure in the last 72 hours with risk 6 or more reported by two or more independent newsrooms - or an official notice in force that speaks of attacks, combat or closure.
  • Elevated: any official notice in force naming the place, an incident with risk 4 or more, or reports at three times their usual variation (24 hours against the 14-day average).
  • Watch: reports clearly above their usual level, or any incident in the last 72 hours. Normal: none of these.
  • Attention is not damage: many reports can mean a story is being discussed, not that ships are affected - read the status together with its reason.

Maritime & Shipping

What you are looking at

  • The status of 12 chokepoints and 15 major ports (normal, watch, elevated, disrupted), set from how much independent newsrooms report about each.
  • Incidents confirmed by two or more newsrooms, and the official notices in force (maritime advisories, navigational warnings, sanctions).

How to use it

  • Click a chokepoint or port card to open everything about it: 15-day chart, incidents and notices. Click again or press Esc to close.
  • Filter incidents by type and notices by kind; copy the address (for example maritime.html#hormuz) to share one place.

Risk colours: gold under 4 · amber 4–6 · orange 6–8 · red 8 and above (scale 0–10).

ATHENA publishes an event only when at least two independent newsrooms report it.
