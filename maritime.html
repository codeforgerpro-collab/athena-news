How the statuses are set

  • Disrupted: an attack, seizure, restriction or port closure in the last 72 hours with risk 6 or more reported by two or more independent newsrooms - or an official notice in force that speaks of attacks, combat or closure.
  • Elevated: any official notice in force naming the place, an incident with risk 4 or more, or reports at three times their usual variation (24 hours against the 14-day average).
  • Watch: reports clearly above their usual level, or any incident in the last 72 hours. Normal: none of these.
  • Attention is not damage: many reports can mean a story is being discussed, not that ships are affected - read the status together with its reason.
