ATHENA News Room is a news-intelligence service, not a newspaper: it gathers the reporting of other newsrooms, groups it into events and explains it with numbers. Read more

Its short texts are written automatically from the linked reports, and every event leads to its original sources. Headlines and links belong to their publishers; ATHENA publishes no article text. Scores are analytical indicators of coverage — not facts, forecasts or probabilities. Spotted an error? Write to us and we will correct it. Built automatically by ATHENA INTELLIGENCE. Automated copying, scraping or bulk downloading of this site is not permitted without written permission. Test edition. Visits are counted anonymously, without cookies and without personal data (GoatCounter).

The front page

What you are looking at

  • The most important verified events of the moment, largest first, each with its risk (0–10) and how many newsrooms report it.
  • The rolling bar under the menu carries breaking events; the boxes further down show today's briefing, risk trackers and warnings.

How to use it

  • Click a headline for the full event: sources, countries, risk history.
  • Use the menu for sections, the Mind Graph, Local news and Maritime.

Risk colours: gold under 4 · amber 4–6 · orange 6–8 · red 8 and above (scale 0–10).

ATHENA publishes an event only when at least two independent newsrooms report it.
