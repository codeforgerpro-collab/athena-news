About this tracker

Greece and Turkey are NATO allies with long-running disagreements over the Aegean, maritime zones in the Eastern Mediterranean and Cyprus. This tracker follows how the world's press covers the relationship, day by day.

How ATHENA measures it

An event belongs to this tracker when its title names the topic, or when at least two of its reports — and at least a third of them — do. Risk is ATHENA's analytical indicator of the coverage (0–10), not a forecast.
