Explained

About this tracker

The Strait of Hormuz links the Persian Gulf to the open ocean. Roughly a fifth of the oil consumed worldwide passes through it, so attacks on shipping or threats to close it move energy prices and insurance costs within hours.

How ATHENA measures it

An event belongs to this tracker when its title names the topic, or when at least two of its reports — and at least a third of them — do. Risk is ATHENA's analytical indicator of the coverage (0–10), not a forecast.
