About this tracker

Beijing claims Taiwan, which governs itself; the United States and its allies watch the Taiwan Strait closely. Military drills, diplomatic moves and chip-industry news here can move markets worldwide.

How ATHENA measures it

An event belongs to this tracker when its title names the topic, or when at least two of its reports — and at least a third of them — do. Risk is ATHENA's analytical indicator of the coverage (0–10), not a forecast.
