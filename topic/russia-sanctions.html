About this tracker

Since 2014, and on a much larger scale since 2022, the EU, the US, the UK and others have imposed sanctions on Russia's banks, energy exports and trade. Changes to them reach energy markets, shipping and companies far beyond Russia.

How ATHENA measures it

An event belongs to this tracker when its title names the topic, or when at least two of its reports — and at least a third of them — do. Risk is ATHENA's analytical indicator of the coverage (0–10), not a forecast.
