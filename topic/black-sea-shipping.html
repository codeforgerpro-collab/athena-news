Explained

About this tracker

The Black Sea carries Ukrainian and Russian grain and Russian oil to world markets; drone and missile attacks on ports and ships and drifting mines make it one of the riskiest seas for merchant shipping.

How ATHENA measures it

An event belongs to this tracker when its title names the topic, or when at least two of its reports — and at least a third of them — do. Risk is ATHENA's analytical indicator of the coverage (0–10), not a forecast.
