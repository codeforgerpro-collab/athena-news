About this tracker

Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine began in February 2022. This tracker follows the fighting, strikes on infrastructure and the diplomacy around the war, as covered by newsrooms around the world.

An event belongs to this tracker when its title names the topic, or when at least two of its reports — and at least a third of them — do. Risk is ATHENA's analytical indicator of the coverage (0–10), not a forecast.
