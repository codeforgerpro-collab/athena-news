Explained

About this tracker

The Red Sea and the Bab el-Mandeb strait form the shortest sea route between Asia and Europe, through the Suez Canal. Attacks on merchant ships push carriers to sail around Africa, adding time and cost to world trade.

How ATHENA measures it

An event belongs to this tracker when its title names the topic, or when at least two of its reports — and at least a third of them — do. Risk is ATHENA's analytical indicator of the coverage (0–10), not a forecast.
