      My ATHENA

      What you are looking at

      • Two lists that belong to you: what you follow (countries, sections, trackers) and the countries you pinned to the World menu.
      • They live only in this browser - nothing is sent anywhere.

      How to use it

      • Stop following or unpin with the button beside each item.
      • Type a country in the box, then press Follow or Pin; switch sections and trackers on with one click.
      • The front page 'For you' box and the World menu use these lists at once.

      Risk colours: gold under 4 · amber 4–6 · orange 6–8 · red 8 and above (scale 0–10).

      ATHENA publishes an event only when at least two independent outlets report it.
