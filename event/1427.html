Ship attacks in Strait of Hormuz hit record high in October

A Washington-based think-tank recorded 28 confirmed and reported attacks. from The Straits Times

In brief

Reported independently by 16 outlets in 10 countries since 10 Oct, first by Asharq Al-Awsat (English); coverage is at its peak. ATHENA rates the risk moderate (3.2/10), driven mainly by the severity of the harm described and its geographic and sector reach. Confidence is 92% (high), with 16 independent outlets corroborating it and an average source reliability of 77%.

Why it matters: The Strait of Hormuz links the Persian Gulf to the open ocean. Roughly a fifth of the oil consumed worldwide passes through it, so attacks on shipping or threats to close it move energy prices and insurance costs within hours.
