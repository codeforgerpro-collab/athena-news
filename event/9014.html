Russia hails Trump waiving sanctions as 'historic'

Trump’s executive order comes as he has been warming towards Russia. from The Straits Times

In brief

Reported independently by 45 outlets in 25 countries since 17 Oct, first by The Straits Times; coverage is at its peak. ATHENA rates the risk elevated (5.9/10), driven mainly by the severity of the harm described and the pace of the reporting. Confidence is 88% (high), with 45 independent outlets corroborating it and an average source reliability of 76%. Who reported it: 4 newsrooms in Ukraine, 3 newsrooms in India, 3 newsrooms in Saudi Arabia. The coverage ran from 17 Oct to 17 Oct, over 9 hours.

Why it matters: Since 2014, and on a much larger scale since 2022, the EU, the US, the UK and others have imposed sanctions on Russia's banks, energy exports and trade. Changes to them reach energy markets, shipping and companies far beyond Russia.
