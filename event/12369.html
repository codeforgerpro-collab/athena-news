Houthis Claim Attacks on Saudi Arabia’s East-West Oil Pipeline

The Iran-backed Houthis on Monday claimed they attacked pumping stations along Saudi Arabia’s East-West onshore oil pipeline, the Kingdom’s key crude export route bypassing the Strait of Hormuz. from OilPrice.com

In brief

Reported independently by 23 outlets in 16 countries since 17 Oct, first by PBS NewsHour; coverage is still spreading. ATHENA rates the risk high (6.8/10), driven mainly by the severity of the harm described and its geographic and sector reach. Confidence is 88% (high), with 23 independent outlets corroborating it and an average source reliability of 77%. Who reported it: 5 newsrooms in Azerbaijan, 3 newsrooms in Pakistan, 3 newsrooms in Turkey. The coverage ran from 17 Oct to 26 Oct, over 207 hours.

Why it matters: The Red Sea and the Bab el-Mandeb strait form the shortest sea route between Asia and Europe, through the Suez Canal. Attacks on merchant ships push carriers to sail around Africa, adding time and cost to world trade.
