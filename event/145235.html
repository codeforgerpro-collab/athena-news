Airstrikes on a market in southwestern Yemen kill at least 30, Houthis say

Footage aired by Al-Masirah showed plumes of smoke rising from the site, with a damaged building containing canned food, destroyed vehicles, scattered fruit boxes and rubble and debris. from Courthouse News

In brief

Reported independently by 13 outlets in 10 countries since 6 Nov, first by AzerNews; coverage is still spreading. ATHENA rates the risk high (6.2/10), driven mainly by the severity of the harm described and the pace of the reporting. Confidence is 92% (high), with 13 independent outlets corroborating it and an average source reliability of 76%. Outlets by country: United States (2), India (2), Malaysia (2). Coverage: 6 Nov to 7 Nov, 22 hours.

Why it matters: The Red Sea and the Bab el-Mandeb strait form the shortest sea route between Asia and Europe, through the Suez Canal. Attacks on merchant ships push carriers to sail around Africa, adding time and cost to world trade.
