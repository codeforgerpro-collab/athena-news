Iran says four tankers on fire after hitting mines in Hormuz

Coalition says it destroyed two ballistic missile launchers The Coalition has released footage it says shows the targeting and destruction of two ballistic missile launch platforms. from Gulf News

In brief

Reported independently by 42 outlets in 25 countries since 17 Oct, first by Gulf News; coverage is at its peak. ATHENA rates the risk moderate (3.3/10), driven mainly by the severity of the harm described and its geographic and sector reach. Confidence is 88% (high), with 42 independent outlets corroborating it and an average source reliability of 77%. Who reported it: 8 newsrooms in Iran, 5 newsrooms in Iraq, 3 newsrooms in Saudi Arabia. The coverage ran from 17 Oct to 20 Oct, over 86 hours.

Why it matters: The Strait of Hormuz links the Persian Gulf to the open ocean. Roughly a fifth of the oil consumed worldwide passes through it, so attacks on shipping or threats to close it move energy prices and insurance costs within hours.
