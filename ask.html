    How to use Ask

    1. Name a place

      A country (Greece, Iran), a region (Middle East, East Asia) or a well-known city. Country and region names work in any of the site's seven languages.

    2. Add a topic or words

      A tracker (Red Sea shipping, Strait of Hormuz), a section (Energy, Technology) or words you expect in a headline: tanker, election, sanctions, earthquake.

    3. Choose a period

      today, last 24 hours, yesterday, last 3 days, this week, this month. Without a period, every event on the site is searched.

    4. Filter by risk, or change the order

      high risk, critical, risk above 7 — and latest, most covered or highest risk to change the order of the answer.

    5. Read the answer

      Each event shows its section, how many outlets in how many countries reported it and ATHENA's risk score. Open it to see who reported it first, every report in order and the reasons behind the score.

    Good to know

    • Headlines stay in English as the outlets published them, so headline words match best in English; places, trackers, sections and periods work in every language.
    • Ask searches the events published on this site — the most important of recent weeks — not every article ATHENA has read.
    • Risk is ATHENA's estimate from 0 to 10 of the harm described in the reporting, with its confidence. It is not a forecast and not advice.
