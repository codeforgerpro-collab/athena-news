How the desk works

Five stages, in a sequence fixed before the evidence is read. Each stage can be checked on its own. The cards above show the answer and the first two stages; an analyst completes stages 3 to 5 and the scores.

  1. 1. Evidence Claims are traced to origin; facts are separated from interpretation (Twelve Principles I-VII).
  2. 2. Case set-up Six inputs are stated: actors, focal actor, trigger, asset space, horizon, prior cases (General Method).
  3. 3. Six mechanisms Honour threshold, deception trap, verification interval, alliance propagation, regime type, culminating point with trinity and friction - and three explanations tested symmetrically: by design, side effect, no design (General Method).
  4. 4. Same instrument Both actors are assessed on seven fixed criteria with the same weights and questions (Isonomia Framework).
  5. 5. Self-review The assessment is checked against all twelve principles; flags stay visible (Twelve Principles I-XII).
