Saudi Arabia suspends travel to Kenya, bans visas and entry over Ebola concerns

Riyadh: Saudi Arabia has suspended travel by its citizens to Kenya, halted the issuance of all types of visas to travellers arriving from the country and banned their entry into the Kingdom as part… من Gulf News

باختصار

لماذا يهم: قد تغيّر التطورات الصحية والعلمية مخاطر الصحة العامة وأولويات البحث والتنظيم.
